# User Manual

## Integrated Court Case Aging & Monitoring System

**Bulwagan ng Katarungan — Catbalogan City**  
**Document version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** November 24, 2026

## 1. Introduction

The Integrated Court Case Aging & Monitoring System (ICCAM) is a web-based application for recording and monitoring cases handled by the Office of the Prosecutor, the Office of the Clerk of Court, and Regional Trial Court (RTC) Branches 27, 28, and 29. It provides role-specific dashboards and tools for case intake, investigation, document management, resolution, court forwarding, docketing, branch assignment, hearings, bail applications, case aging, and reports.

This manual explains how to use the functions currently available in the system. It is an operational guide, not legal advice or a substitute for official court rules, office procedures, or approval authority. Follow applicable procedures when entering, reviewing, approving, or releasing case records and documents.

## 2. User Roles

The pages and actions available after login depend on the account role.

| Role | Main responsibilities in the system |
|---|---|
| **Fiscal / Prosecutor** | Record complaints, manage case records and evidence, conduct investigation and review, prepare resolutions, and forward cases to the Clerk of Court. |
| **Clerk of Court** | Review forwarded cases, record criminal docket numbers, raffle cases to RTC branches, and perform Clerk-level bail processing actions. |
| **Branch user** | Work with cases assigned to the user's branch, maintain hearing records, handle branch-level bail actions, review documents, and view aging information and reports. |

The Clerk of Court manages cases for the court, while a Branch account is associated with a particular branch. Use only the account assigned to you; do not share login credentials.

## 3. Accessing the System

1. Open the system address provided by the system administrator in a supported web browser.
2. On the login page, enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Select **Login**. The system opens the dashboard associated with your account role.
4. To finish a session, select **Logout** in the navigation menu.

The application does not provide public account registration. If you do not have an account, cannot sign in, or need a password reset, contact the system administrator. If the system reports that the database is unavailable, contact the administrator rather than repeatedly submitting the login form.

## 4. General Navigation

- The **Dashboard** is the starting page and displays role-specific case or workflow information.
- Use the sidebar to open the modules available to your role.
- Lists commonly provide search fields, filters, status labels, or actions for opening a record.
- A case's current status indicates its place in the workflow. Complete required earlier steps before attempting a later step.
- Review entries before saving or approving them. Generated previews and documents should be checked for accuracy before printing, downloading, or forwarding.
- The same case may appear in different modules as it progresses through the system. Use its case or docket number to verify that you are working on the intended record.

## 5. Fiscal / Prosecutor User Guide

### 5.1 Dashboard

Open **Dashboard** to review overall case totals and the distribution of cases by status. Use the sidebar to proceed to intake, case management, or another available module.

### 5.2 Create a case

1. Open **Case Intake**.
2. In **Enter Data**, complete the complaint information presented on the form. This includes office-received information, complainant and respondent details, the alleged offense and applicable law, incident and case facts, and any witness information requested.
3. Enter information from the source records. Provide accurate spelling, dates, contact details, and case facts; do not guess when information is unknown.
4. Open **Preview Documents** and review the generated document previews for completeness and accuracy.
5. Open **Evidence Upload** and attach the relevant files. Use clear, descriptive names and select the correct files.
6. Submit the intake form after reviewing the data and attachments. Confirm that the case appears in the case list.

Some docket or office-use fields may be populated by the system or configured settings. Do not alter read-only or automatically assigned values.

### 5.3 Find and review cases

1. Open **Case List**.
2. Search by case number, docket number, or party name, or select a status filter.
3. Open the appropriate case action to view the record, continue investigation, prepare a resolution, or access court forwarding where available.
4. Use **Documents** to search and review documents associated with cases. Use available view or download controls only for authorized work.

### 5.4 Investigation and review

1. Open **Investigation & Review** and select the relevant case.
2. Review the case information and existing documents.
3. Use **Add Witness** to record witness information where appropriate.
4. Use **Upload Document** to add case-related files, selecting an appropriate document type and name.
5. Use the available investigation actions, such as issuing a subpoena, recording receipt of a counter-affidavit, or allowing a reply, when the case workflow calls for them.
6. Review the case materials before selecting **Complete Investigation**. The case may then proceed to resolution according to its status.

### 5.5 Prepare a resolution

1. Open **Resolution** for a case ready for review.
2. Select the applicable decision presented by the system: **File**, **Dismiss**, or **Reinvestigate**.
3. Enter clear findings and rationale in the designated field.
4. Review the resolution preview and confirm the case details and rationale.
5. Use the approval action only when authorized. If the resolution requires changes, use the return-for-revision action and provide revision notes.

### 5.6 Forward a case to court

1. Open **Court Forwarding** and select a case available in the forwarding queue.
2. Review the filing-document checklist and the available case documents. The checklist includes documents such as the resolution, criminal information, complaint affidavit, investigation form, counter-affidavit, evidence, and endorsement.
3. Generate or review the required document previews using the available actions.
4. Verify the documents against the source records and applicable office procedure.
5. Complete the sworn-document certification step when required by the interface, then select **Forward to Clerk**.
6. Confirm that the case status or forwarding record reflects the completed action.

The system's checklist and document-generation features assist with preparation; they do not replace the required legal or supervisory review.

### 5.7 Settings

Open **Settings** to view the configuration options provided to your account, such as court, prosecutor, or document details. Change office-wide settings only if authorized.

## 6. Clerk of Court User Guide

### 6.1 Dashboard and docket list

The dashboard summarizes docketing and raffle activity. Open **Docket List** to review cases forwarded from Fiscal. Use the status filters to focus on pending docket, docketed, or raffled records.

### 6.2 Record a case in the docket

1. In **Docket List**, locate the forwarded case and use **View Docs** to review its documents.
2. Select **Docket** for a case that has not yet been assigned to a branch.
3. Review the suggested criminal case number. Edit it only when the official docket register requires a different number.
4. Enter or confirm the docket date and select **Record Docket**.
5. Verify the updated record and its docket status.

### 6.3 Raffle a docketed case

1. Open **Raffle Cases** and select a case ready for assignment.
2. Use **START RAFFLE** for the random raffle. Review the displayed branch and judge, then select **CONFIRM ASSIGNMENT** to record the result. Use **Retry** only before confirming if the displayed result should not be recorded.
3. If office procedure authorizes a direct assignment, use the manual branch-assignment option instead.
4. Confirm the final assigned branch in the case or branch-status information.

The raffle interface lists RTC Branches 27, 28, and 29. Follow the court's official assignment rules and authorization requirements.

### 6.4 Branch status

Open **Branches 27, 28, 29** to review branch information and case assignments. The page may provide branch-specific case summaries and judge assignment controls. Update judge information only when authorized and verify the saved assignment.

### 6.5 Bail applications

Open **Bail Applications** to review applications and their current stage. Available actions depend on the application's status and the user's role. Clerk-level steps may include verifying submitted documents and recording payment method, official receipt number, and amount. Enter details from the official records and verify them before saving.

## 7. Branch User Guide

### 7.1 Dashboard

The branch dashboard summarizes assigned cases, upcoming hearings, and other branch-level information. Use **View All** or the sidebar to open the related case or hearing list.

### 7.2 Case list and case details

1. Open **Case List** to review cases assigned to your branch.
2. Search by case number, title, party, or offense, and apply status filters as needed.
3. Use **View** to open a case and review its details and related information.
4. Use the archived-case filter only when you need to include archived records.

### 7.3 Schedule and record hearings

1. Open **Hearings** and select **Schedule Hearing**.
2. Enter the case, date, time, hearing type, room, and any relevant notes requested by the form.
3. Save the schedule and verify it appears under the correct case.
4. Use **By Case** to review hearings grouped by case or **Calendar** to view dates by month.
5. For an upcoming hearing, use **Record Proceedings** after the hearing to record the proceeding details, attendance, and outcome requested by the system.
6. Review the saved hearing record. Correct or update information only through the available authorized actions.

### 7.4 Bail applications

Open **Bail Applications** to review applications associated with your branch. Depending on the case status, branch-level actions may include recording the judge's approval and bail amount, denying an application with a reason, or submitting the required documents. Follow the displayed stage labels and handoff instructions.

The workflow may also require Clerk-level document verification and recording of payment and receipt details before a release order can be issued. Issue or print a release order only when the system indicates that the application has reached the appropriate stage and the action is authorized.

### 7.5 Case aging and documents

- Open **Case Aging** to search and filter branch cases by the available case, status, or aging categories. Select **View** to inspect a case.
- Open **Documents** to search or filter by case and document type. Use the available view or download buttons to access authorized documents.
- Keep document descriptions and case references accurate so that records can be identified correctly.

### 7.6 Reports

Open **Reports** to view the branch dashboard, generate available summaries, or export selected records. Available reports include monthly case, hearing schedule, and judgment summaries. For data export, apply the available status, offense, and date filters, select the intended cases, then choose CSV or PDF export. Check the selection and date range before exporting.

### 7.7 Settings

Where **Settings** is available to your account, use it to manage the options provided there. If changing your password, enter the current password, enter the new password, and confirm it. The form requires a minimum of eight characters. Keep the new password private.

## 8. Bail Application Workflow Overview

The application displays bail applications in stages. The exact actions available depend on the status and the user's role.

1. **Pending:** The branch records the judge's approval and amount, or records a denial with its reason.
2. **Granted:** The required documents can be submitted through the branch workflow.
3. **Documents submitted:** The application awaits Clerk-level document review.
4. **Documents verified:** The application awaits Clerk-level payment and receipt recording.
5. **Payment processed:** The branch can proceed with the release-order action when authorized.
6. **Posted:** The release order can be opened or printed using the available control.
7. **Denied:** The application is marked denied.

Status labels and controls in the application are the primary guide to the next available step. Coordinate handoffs between the Branch and Clerk as required by office procedure.

## 9. Common Case Workflow

The system supports the following high-level progression:

**Fiscal intake → investigation and review → resolution → court forwarding → Clerk docketing → raffle to a branch → branch case and hearing management → reporting and case monitoring**

Not every record follows an identical path, and the available actions depend on the current case status and user role. If a case is not available in a module, confirm its current status and required prior steps with the responsible office.

## 10. Good Record-Keeping Practices

- Enter information from verified source documents and review it before saving.
- Use accurate case numbers and party names when searching or entering information.
- Upload readable files under the correct case and document type.
- Review generated document previews before printing, downloading, approving, or forwarding.
- Record hearing outcomes, bail details, and receipt information accurately and promptly.
- Treat case records and uploaded documents as confidential. Access or export only information needed for authorized duties.
- Log out when finished, especially on a shared workstation.
- For an error, missing record, incorrect status, or access issue, note the case number and the screen/action involved, then contact the system administrator or responsible office. Do not create a duplicate record to work around an error.
